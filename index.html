Key Features

Ultra-Low Power Innovations

Designed to achieve zero-energy standby states, combining energy harvesting with Texas Instruments MSP430 LPM4.5 power modes.

Zero Standby Power

Node main micro-controller remains in deep LPM4.5 power mode (sub-100nA draw) until an RF pattern triggers the wake-up interrupt.

Sub-GHz RF Receiver

High-sensitivity passive envelope detector rectifying sub-GHz radio waves to generate a physical hardware interrupt signal.

MSP430 Integration

Texas Instruments 16-Bit FRAM microcontroller offering fast wake-up recovery time (<4.5μs) and non-volatile state retention.

Energy Harvesting

Solar and ambient RF energy harvester module charging ultra-capacitors for infinite maintenance-free deployment.

Cloud IoT Telemetry

High-speed burst telemetry transmission over LoRa/FSK following sensor payload acquisition before returning to sleep.

Hardware Address Demux

16-bit address matching logic verifying incoming wake-up packet sequence to prevent false wakeups from ambient noise.

Project Milestones

Development Timeline

Key engineering milestones achieved during the design and deployment of the Zero-Energy Receiver Network.

PHASE 1

RF Receiver Circuit Simulation

Designed zero-bias Schottky diode envelope detector schematics in SPICE and optimized matching networks for 868MHz ISM band.

PHASE 2

MSP430 LPM4.5 Firmware Optimization

Configured Texas Instruments MSP430FR5994 FRAM MCU for deep LPM4.5 low-power mode with fast NMI interrupt wake-up vector.

PHASE 3

Energy Harvesting Integration

Interfaced solar photovoltaic cells and RF energy harvester ICs with supercapacitor power management modules.

PHASE 4

Field Deployment & Range Testing

Deployed physical sensor nodes in outdoor environments, verifying reliable 100m+ RF wake-up trigger range and zero standby loss.
